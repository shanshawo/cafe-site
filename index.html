Our story

Started with a borrowed grinder and a stubborn idea.

We opened in 2021 with one machine, one grinder (borrowed), and a wall that needed painting twice because we argued about the colour. The idea was simple: a café that feels like a kitchen, not a showroom.

The coffee comes from a small roastery we've worked with since day one. The food is cooked by people who actually eat it. Nothing on the menu exists just to photograph well, though most of it does anyway.

You'll find us on a side street off the main road, behind the tree that's slowly taking over the pavement. Come for the coffee. Stay because nobody's rushing you out.
